Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The Check In ("the app") is a personal daily check-in tool. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

What information we collect

The app stores the names and phone numbers of contacts that the app owner manually enters, solely for the purpose of sending daily "I'm okay" check-in text messages.

How we use information

Phone numbers are used only to send SMS check-in notifications via Twilio. We do not sell, share, or use this information for marketing or any other purpose.

Data storage

Contact information is stored securely and is only accessible to the app owner.

Opting out

Any contact may ask the app owner at any time to be removed from the check-in list, and their information will be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner directly.
