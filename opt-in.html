The Check In — Opt-In Documentation

How end users consent to SMS alerts

This page documents the messaging opt-in flow for carrier / A2P 10DLC review. It exists for verification purposes and is publicly viewable — no login required.

Opt-in method

Contact added by app owner → SMS confirmation request

The Check In lets a user (the "app owner") name up to five people who should be notified when they check in daily. When the app owner adds a new contact's phone number, that number does not start receiving messages automatically. Instead, our system sends a single one-time SMS asking the contact to actively confirm.

  1. App owner enters a contact's name and phone number inside The Check In app, shown below exactly as it appears in production.
  2. Saving triggers a one-time SMS to that phone number, asking them to reply YES to opt in. No recurring messages are sent yet.
  3. The contact must reply YES of their own accord. This is an unchecked, opt-in-by-reply flow — consent is never assumed or pre-selected on the contact's behalf.
  4. Only after a YES reply is logged does that number become active in the app owner's check-in notification list.
  5. If the contact does not reply, or replies with anything other than YES, no further messages are sent to that number.

Exact message sent at opt-in

This app's A2P 10DLC campaign is pending carrier approval, so live delivery is not yet active. The message below is the exact wording our system sends via the Twilio API once a contact is added — confirmed directly from Twilio's own message logs, not a mockup of hoped-for copy.
SMS · sent once per newly added contact
Robert added you as a check-in contact on The Check In. Reply YES to confirm you'll receive daily check-in alerts from them. Reply STOP to opt out.
YES
You're confirmed! You'll now receive check-in alerts. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

"Robert" is dynamically replaced with the name of the person who added the contact.

Ongoing messages after opt-in

Once a contact has replied YES, they'll receive a short SMS notification only when the app owner presses their daily check-in button. Example:

This is my daily check-in from The Check In app — I'm okay!

Opt-out

Any contact can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, and HELP for assistance, consistent with standard carrier requirements and Twilio's built-in opt-out management.
